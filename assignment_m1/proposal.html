Final Project Proposal

About Project

My website will be a pet adoption site, where there will be different pet breeds or either cats or dogs the user can choose from. If there is time, they may purchase pet clothing accessories.

Intended Audience

The intended audience are dog or cat lovers looking to adopt a pet that needs a home!

Design & Style

The colors will be inspired by existing shopping websites provided below, and a color palette of pastel colors. The layout will have divide the screen (that is not the login page) into sections (navigation, body, and footer). The body will be divided into two columns (hopefully responsive) and contain the pets ready for adoption in a 3 by 3 grid. When the user hovers over the image, they can click and it will zoom into several detail, giving the user the option to add to cart.

Scope

I am plannning to have 3 pages (add a 4th if there is time).

  • Login/Create Account
  • Shopping
  • Cart + Checkout
  • About the Creator

Database

The dog's breed, unique id, name, and short fees for the vacinations will be stored in the database. The source of my data will come from the users who decide to create an account, and self-populated data.
